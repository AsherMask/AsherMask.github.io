# Bouncing Box Bonus Challenges

All six challenges are implemented in `index.html`.

1. **Custom background and box styling** — the board uses a layered gradient
   background, and the box has a highlight and shadow.
2. **Vertical movement** — the box moves and bounces on both axes, including
   after a window resize.
3. **Random position on every click** — each successful click places the box
   at a random point within the play area.
4. **Click-driven colors** — the box receives a new random color on every
   successful click.
5. **Increasing speed boosts** — the box speeds up on each hit, and the amount
   added increases every three points.
6. **Ten-miss game over** — background clicks count as misses; the game stops
   at ten and offers a restart. Clicking the box does not count as a miss.

## Check the challenges

- Click the box and confirm the score, color, position, and speed change.
- Confirm it bounces off the left, right, top, and bottom edges.
- Click the background ten times and use **Play again** to start a fresh round.
